# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Daysteps is designed for children and collects **no personal data**.

- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.** Daysteps contains no
  advertising or analytics code.
- **Everything stays on the device.** Boards, settings and photos you add are
  stored only in the app's private storage on your device (or your browser's
  storage for the web app). Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data,
  deletes them.
- **Camera and photos** are used only when an adult chooses to take or pick a
  photo for a card, or to scan a Daysteps QR code. Images are not uploaded
  anywhere.
- **Sharing is manual.** Boards leave the device only when an adult shares a
  file or shows a QR code.
- **Internet** is used only if an adult searches or downloads ARASAAC
  pictograms. Those requests go directly to `api.arasaac.org` and
  `static.arasaac.org` and contain only the search words. Opening the web app
  loads it from GitHub Pages.
- **Speech** uses the text-to-speech engine already on your device or in your
  browser. Some browsers may use an online voice service of their own.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Daysteps/issues.
